      Limitations

      Source attribution
      A sound level meter records total sound level and cannot identify the source. Source-specific guideline values (aircraft, railway) are shown for reference only.
      Health guidelines and legal limits
      WHO 2018 values are health-based guidelines; COMAR 26.02.03 values are regulatory limits. Meeting a regulatory limit does not imply meeting the health-based guideline.
      Instrument
      Results depend on microphone placement, windscreen use and calibration of the monitoring hardware.
      Intended use
      Environmental and epidemiological research. Not a clinical assessment or legal determination.
